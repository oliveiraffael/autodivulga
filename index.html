AutoDivulga AI

AutoDivulga AI is a free desktop application (Windows), developed by Raffael Oliveira, that creators install on their own computer to schedule, publish and measure their own videos on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

What it does

  • Organizes video campaigns: title, caption, hashtags, script and the local video file.
  • Writes caption drafts and video ideas with a local AI; the creator reviews everything before publishing.
  • Keeps a publishing calendar and, at the scheduled time, publishes the creator's own videos to the creator's own accounts through the official APIs (YouTube Data API, TikTok Content Posting API, Meta Graph API).
  • Shows views, likes, comments and shares of the creator's videos, a comments inbox and weekly recommendations.

How it works

The app runs only on the creator's computer (local web panel at 127.0.0.1). Each creator signs in to each network through the platform's own consent screen. Access tokens are stored only on that computer and can be removed at any time with the "Disconnect" button or in the platform's settings. The app has no central server and sends no data to the developer.

Who uses it

Independent creators managing their own channels and profiles. It has no ads, does not sell data and never accesses accounts that the user did not connect.
