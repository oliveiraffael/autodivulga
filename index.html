AutoDivulga AI

AutoDivulga AI is a free, personal desktop application (Windows) that I, Raffael Oliveira, built to manage the publishing of my own animated storytelling videos on my YouTube channel, @ooliveiraffael.

What it does

  • Organizes my video campaigns: title, description, hashtags and the local video file.
  • Writes caption drafts that I review and edit before anything is published.
  • Keeps a publishing calendar and, at the time I schedule, uploads my own videos to my own channel through the official YouTube Data API (videos.insert, thumbnails.set).
  • Shows the view, like and comment counts of the videos I published, so I can see what works (videos.list, channels.list).

How it works

The app runs only on my computer (local web panel at 127.0.0.1). I sign in with my Google account through Google's OAuth consent screen. The access token is stored only on my computer and can be removed at any time with the "Disconnect" button or at Google Account permissions. No data is sent to any server owned by me or by third parties.

Who uses it

It is a single-user tool for my own channel. It is not sold, has no ads and does not access other people's channels.
