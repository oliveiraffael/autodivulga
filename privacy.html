Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AutoDivulga AI ("the app") accesses, uses, stores and deletes information. AutoDivulga AI is a free desktop application (Windows) developed by Raffael Oliveira that each creator installs and runs on their own computer to schedule, publish and measure their own videos on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

1. Platform services used

AutoDivulga AI uses YouTube API Services, the TikTok for Developers APIs (Login Kit, Content Posting API and Display API) and the Meta Graph API (Facebook Pages and Instagram API with Facebook Login). By using these features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, the TikTok Terms of Service and the Meta Terms. Information obtained through these services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy), the TikTok Privacy Policy and the Meta Privacy Policy.

2. Information the app accesses

The app only accesses the accounts that you connect yourself, with the permissions you approve:

  • YouTube (youtube.upload, youtube.readonly, optionally youtube.force-ssl): upload videos you choose, read your channel name, picture and statistics (views, likes, comments) and, if you enable the Comments inbox, read the comments on your videos and post the replies you write.
  • TikTok (user.info.basic, video.upload, optionally user.info.stats, video.list): send videos as drafts to your TikTok inbox and read your profile and video statistics.
  • Facebook and Instagram (pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts, read_insights, business_management, instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish, instagram_manage_insights): list the Pages you manage and the Instagram professional account linked to them, publish the Reels you schedule, read their statistics and, if you use the trends feature, read public top posts of hashtags you choose.

The app also stores the information you type into it: titles, captions, hashtags, schedule dates, scripts and the path of video files on your computer.

3. How the information is used

  • To publish the videos you scheduled, with the text and settings you approved.
  • To show your statistics, recommendations and a weekly report inside the app (and, if you connect it, in your own Telegram bot).
  • The app does not use the data for advertising, does not sell it and does not create profiles of people.

4. Where the information is stored

All data is stored only on the user's own computer, inside the app folder (database autodivulga.db, access tokens such as youtube_token.json, tiktok_token.json and meta_token.json, and the contas folder with other accounts you saved). The app has no central server: nothing is sent to the developer. The only external communication is with the platforms above and, optionally, with services you configure yourself (a local or remote AI to draft captions, which receives only the text you typed; your Telegram bot; your own Cloudflare account for partner pop-ups).

5. Sharing

The app does not share, sell or transfer any user data or platform API data to third parties.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

The local panel uses a single session cookie, stored in your browser, only to show messages between pages. It does not place tracking cookies, does not allow third parties to place or read cookies and does not collect device information.

7. Data retention and refresh

Statistics obtained from the platforms are refreshed about every 6 hours while the app is running, and the daily history is kept for at most 30 days. Comments read from YouTube are deleted after 30 days without refresh. Switching to another account deletes the stored statistics of the previous one.

8. How to revoke access and delete your data

  • Revoke access in the app: click "Disconnect" for each network in the Accounts page. This revokes the access at the platform when supported and deletes the token from your computer.
  • Revoke at the platform: Google account connections, TikTok app → Settings → Security → Apps and services, and Facebook Business Integrations / Instagram → Settings → Website permissions.
  • Delete data: disconnecting deletes the token immediately; deleting the app folder deletes everything. Because no data is stored by the developer, there is no remote copy to delete. If you need help, email raffaelquemsou@gmail.com and you will receive the instructions above within 30 days.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and is meant to be used by the owner of each connected account.
