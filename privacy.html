Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AutoDivulga AI ("the app") accesses, uses, stores and deletes information. The app is a personal desktop tool operated by Raffael Oliveira.

1. Use of YouTube API Services

AutoDivulga AI uses YouTube API Services. By using the app's YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Information obtained through YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

2. Information the app accesses

When you connect your Google account, the app requests these permissions (OAuth scopes):

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos you choose to your channel and set their thumbnails.
  • youtube.readonly: to read your channel name, picture and public statistics, and the statistics (views, likes, comments) of videos published with the app.

The app also stores the information you type into it: video titles, descriptions, hashtags, schedule dates and the path of video files on your computer.

3. How the information is used

  • To upload the videos you scheduled, with the title, description and settings you approved.
  • To show your channel and video statistics inside the app.
  • The app does not use the data for advertising, does not sell it and does not create profiles of people.

4. Where the information is stored

All data is stored only on your own computer, inside the app folder: the database autodivulga.db, the access token youtube_token.json and a cache of channel information youtube_channel.json. The app has no server: nothing is sent to the developer or to third parties. The only external communication is with Google/YouTube APIs (and, optionally, with an AI text service configured by you to draft captions, which receives only the title, topic and audience you typed, never YouTube data).

5. Sharing

The app does not share, sell or transfer any user data or YouTube API data to third parties.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

The local panel uses a single session cookie, stored in your browser, only to show messages between pages. It does not place tracking cookies or allow third parties to place or read cookies, and it does not collect device information.

7. Data retention and refresh

Channel information from YouTube API Services is refreshed at least every 6 hours while the app is running and is never kept for more than 30 days without being refreshed. Public statistics are kept only while the related publication exists in the app.

8. How to revoke access and delete your data

  • Revoke access: click "Disconnect" in the app's Accounts page (this revokes the token at Google and deletes it from your computer), or remove "AutoDivulga" at Google security settings.
  • Delete data: disconnecting deletes the token and the cached channel information immediately. Deleting a publication deletes its stored statistics. Deleting the app folder deletes everything.
  • If access is revoked at Google, the app deletes the stored token and cached YouTube data the next time it tries to use them, and in any case within 7 days.
  • You can also request help to delete data by email: raffaelquemsou@gmail.com.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children and is meant to be used by the channel owner.

10. Changes

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new date.
